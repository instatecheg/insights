CEO Company Sources

Add an ERPNext database without writing SQL. All companies must use SAR.

Open CEO dashboard
Registration includes all companies in the selected database. Combined cards, comparisons, a separate source summary and synchronization are configured automatically. Totals are management figures, without intercompany eliminations.

1. Connect the database

Use a read-only database user. In Insights, choose MariaDB, enter the credentials, enable SSL, and save. Return here afterward.

Open Insights Data Sources

2. Select and check the company source

3. Register

Imports GL Entry, Account and Company, binds the financial query to this database, and adds it to the dashboard. A database can be registered only once.

4. Review and refresh

Wait for Registered, then reload the CEO dashboard and click Refresh. It synchronizes every registered source. Hourly imports also remain enabled.

Disconnect archives a source from managed reporting without deleting its connection or charts. Other queries and scheduled imports are not disabled. Use Reactivate to restore it, then reload dashboards, clear old company selections and Refresh for current data. Finish pending registration or Refresh jobs before changing membership.
